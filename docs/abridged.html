Abridged By Us

Season 1 · Sci-Fi & Fantasy · 17 episodes

Currently publishing audio/video fan art with AI to bait people into digesting story shapes and become willing to read on their own. At minimum, I hope to trigger reminders in humans of their agency, once they understand what they’re watching.

“Abridged By Us” is about making us pay more proactive attention to the culture we hope to categorically withhold from AI via post-modern copyright.

Each episode reads a book’s Wikipedia plot summary straight-faced, one sentence per slide, over art that “AI” picked for that sentence. Every Wikipedia link points at the revision that was live when the video was made — several of them changed within days. The whole series is a time capsule.
